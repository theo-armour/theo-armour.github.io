# 2026-10-27 The Menagerie

I have been writing little tools for exploring GitHub since 2016. They live in an organization called [Pushme-Pullyou](https://github.com/pushme-pullyou) — named, with a spelling of my own, after the two-headed pushmi-pullyu in the Doctor Dolittle books, because so much of Git is pushing and pulling.

This week I admitted I was lost in my own zoo. There was TooToo, and TooToo Hub, and GubGub, and a fourth thing with no name, and I could not say in one sentence how they related. So I sat down with my robot friend and we sorted it out.

It turns out they were never four different ideas. They are one idea looked at through four widths of lens:

- **TooToo** looks at *one repo*. Drop a single HTML file into any repo and the repo becomes a browsable site. Too-Too is the Doctor's owl, the accountant — he keeps the books of one repo.
- **DabDab** looks at *one user or organization* — all the repos, organizations and stars of a whole GitHub household. Dab-Dab is the duck, the housekeeper.
- **GubGub** looks at *all of GitHub* — whiz around and see what anyone is up to. Gub-Gub is the pig, curious and forever rooting about.
- **JipJip** looks at *right now* — the issues, pull requests and actions in flight. Jip is the dog whose miraculous nose always finds where the action is.

Three widths of *where*, and one lens of *when*. Who, what, when, where — the same questions I keep asking about everything else I have ever made.

The animals share almost all their code. Each one is the one before it plus another layer, so they are all assembled from one source tree — a workshop with two heads, like its namesake: one head pulls improvements in, the other pushes finished single-file builds out to each animal's own repo. The workshop will be called **Pushme-Pullyou** too, when it earns its own repo. The organization is the family, the site is the front door, the workshop is where the work happens.

And the old site? Ten years of earlier TooToos and the first GubGub are not deleted — a budding ancestor does not burn the family albums. The 2016–2019 site now lives whole at [/2019/](https://pushme-pullyou.github.io/2019/), and the new front door quietly forwards old links there.

The new front door is here:

<https://pushme-pullyou.github.io/>

Only TooToo is fully alive today. DabDab is half-born inside this very website. But for the first time in a decade, I know what each animal is for, what it is called, and where it will live.

Polynesia the parrot — the one who taught the Doctor to speak the animal languages — is still in the drawer. I am saving her for the day one of these tools learns to talk.
