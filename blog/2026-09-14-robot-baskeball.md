# 2026-09-14 Robot Basketball

Humans vs Robots Basketball / https://substack.com/home/post/p-214059043

Kevin has a bet against robots being able to play basketball better than humans by 2040.

He provides great logic as to how he will win, that it will still be too difficult for the robots to win.

But has Kevin ever considered my team of misaligned robots?

A defense robot stands next to the robots' basket. Its ten foot arm intercepts balls before they get into the basket. Then my robot hurls balls at 100 mph to the other end of the court.

Next to the humans’ basket, my offene robot catches 100 mph balls and dunks the balls into the human's basket.

Roving around the court, another robot with a ten foot arm snares balls that human players are passing to each other. When the bot gets a ball it sends it down to the offense robot.

Game over for the humans, Kevin?
