# 2026-10-25 Friendship

One meets many, many, many people during one's lifetime. Very occasionally, one or more of them become friends. Most people you meet don't become your friends.People that are your friends don't always last a lifetime.

So what makes a person a friend?

Proximity
I think it's probably hard to make a friend you've never met or who lives at a distance and you'll never meet. Yes, we know about pen pals and long-term letter-corresponding friends. Éloïse and Abelard come to mind. But that is rare.It's probably a special case that needs more consideration.And probably does not last forever, an entire lifetime

Enthusiasm
What sparks a friendship is probably that there needs to be something that both parties are enthusiastic about.The two agree or feel they agree about the goodness of something or things.

It's not a lot of fun having a friend you always disagree with.

You say something, or they say something, and eyes light up. There is a meeting of the minds.

Frequency
I think there needs to be a fair amount of interaction, but some of it could be online or by telephone. At least occasionally, some of it must be in person. 

Trust
Trust is kind of weird. You may have a friend that is totally untrustworthy. They say one thing and do another. They say they'll be there on time, and they're not. Maybe they're late, maybe they're early. Yet somehow they are still your friend.
