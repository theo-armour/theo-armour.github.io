# 2026-08-31 The Real Revolution

https://www.swiss-miss.com/2026/06/the-real-revolution.html


“I still think the [real] revolution is to make the world safe for poetry, meandering, for the frail and vulnerable, the rare and obscure, the impractical and local and small.”
– Rebecca Solnit

This quote adds a new thought to what has been one of my long-standing personal thrusts which is to follow my high school prayer (even if I am an atheist) which is "to be kind and unselfish in friendship, mindful of those less happy than myself, and eager to bear the burdens of others"

"Grant, O Lord, that in all the joys of life we may never forget to be kind. Help us to be unselfish in friendship, mindful of those less happy than ourselves, and eager to bear the burdens of others, through Jesus Christ our Savior. Amen."

Much as I may follow and go along with all of these thoughts, they are about unhappy things, not about happy things. They are about distress rather than beauty. More about poverty than psychological richness.

And given that I'm always looking for a positive spin on things, this "real revolution" quote really is, to an extent, perhaps what I'm been looking for, or a more positive way of looking at the same issues:

What are you trying to do? What is meaningful to you? What makes you happy?

Given that I am partially all of the above in the quote, this resonates, and I should try and make this an additional part of my vision.

Thank you, Swiss Miss.
