# 2026-09-13 Third Brain

In 2025, I noted in my diary today: "People are smarter when they write than when they talk."

Today I will add to that thought: People are even smarter when they have a third brain! When they are writing or thinking about a topic. The topic could be:

- raising dogs
- playing bridge
- programming software
- any number of things
- playing tennis

But perhaps it's a body of knowledge that people can relate to, like a memory palace that Really gets the brain cooking. 

***

I'm thinking of a member of a kennel club, and there are so many things that you can see when you're around a bunch of dogs:
- their attention
- their bark
- the coat of fur
- what their feet look like
All sorts of things. Each of these is a reminder, as you walk around, of a certain aspects of a huge body of dog knowledge.

For me, it was looking at a thousand lines of code, each representing a zillion little thoughts, and I don't have to keep them all in my brain. They just pop in and out almost instantaneously, so it's as if I'm having a huge body of knowledge in my brain at once. It's not really multitasking, but it's processing so much stuff so quickly that it's all in the cache at the same time. And so quickly accessibleIt's as if all the bits were in my conscious that could only handle a few pieces of information all at once. 

So, something like being in the flow means you have to be in the flow in something, in some kind of environment or some way of working or being. 

So, in the same way, I might sit through a Jacques Lacan lecture and not understand a thing. But if I were practicing psychoanalysis and had listened to Lacan and all my colleagues all waffling on about the same topics in the same way, then I would get the picture (because all of these things were popping into my brain at this, and I knew most of them and where most of them came from). 

But not being a practicing psychoanalyst, Riemann was a great philosopher. Most of these thoughts wash over me, and I have little deep meaning. 

And I think this is actually okay. I probably will never be a deep programmer again. I shouldn't plan to be a member of a kennel club. I certainly am not around enough philosophers to have ongoing deep engagements on their topics either. And I guess I'm saying this is okay. 

That living a simple, in-the-moment, aware life, imagining the past, imagining the future but living in the right now is fine. 

I can be lewis Carroll's Humpty Dumpty, and a word I say means whatever I want it to mean. 

But then what is my third brain? Is it all these blog posts? 

Of course. It just popped in my brain. What I would need to build for my archive is a new version of the 3D operating system I was playing with it back in 2009 during my UCB internet media course with Christiana Paul. 

My memory palace is a 3D globe that's basically an infinite 2D map wrapped on a globe. It's also a timeline with a slider somewhere that you go back and forwards in time. What you see is the drawing of the future. It can be sort of 3D images, like what the architect sees. It can be structured the way the structural engineer sees It can be schematics, the way the HVAC person looks at it. 

Or another way of putting it: it's a graph that can take arbitrary physical shapes.
